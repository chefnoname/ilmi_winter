Why winter

Winter is the easiest season to build a habit

The days are short, the nights are long, and there's less pulling you out of the house. This year winter runs straight into Ramadan, so whatever you lock in now, you carry in with you.

NightDay 7h 50mNight

London, 21 December: under 8 hours of daylight and over 16 hours of night. A fast that day lasts about 10 hours.

What you'll leave with

Three winter habits, each with a first step for the next day

  • Fasting

    Fast while the days are short

    A December fast in the UK is about 10 hours. It's the gentlest practice run you'll get before Ramadan.

    Start this weekPick your day tonight. In winter it's over almost before you feel hungry.

  • Nights

    Put the long nights to work

    Sixteen hours of darkness leaves room for a few quiet minutes before bed or before dawn. You'll learn how to claim them and keep them.

    Start tonightTake five quiet minutes before you sleep, with your phone in another room. On the night you'll learn what to fill them with.

  • Mornings and afternoons

    Guard the two ends of the day

    When the day is this short, the early morning and the late afternoon carry it. Hold those two and the rest follows.

    Start tomorrowSet your alarm for before sunrise tonight, and get up when it goes off.

Why winter

Winter, the believer's spring

For the Salaf, winter was the easiest season of worship in the whole year. This year it comes right before Ramadan, so every fast and every night prayer you start now is practice for the month itself.

NightDay 7h 50mNight

London, 21 December: under 8 hours of daylight and over 16 hours of night. A December fast there lasts about 10 hours.

مَرْحَبًا بِالشِّتَاءِ، تَنْزِلُ فِيهِ الْبَرَكَةُ، وَيَطُولُ فِيهِ اللَّيْلُ لِلْقِيَامِ، وَيَقْصُرُ فِيهِ النَّهَارُ لِلصِّيَامِ

"Welcome, winter. Blessings come down in it. Its night is long for standing in prayer, and its day is short for fasting."

Reported from Ibn Mas'ud Ustadh to check

What you'll leave with

3 habits of the Salaf, each with a step you can start tomorrow

  • Fasting

    One voluntary fast this week

    "Fasting in winter is the cool, easy booty."Abu Hurayrah (Tirmidhi) Ustadh to check

    Start tomorrowPick your fast day for this week tonight. In winter the day is over almost before you feel hungry.

  • Night prayer

    Qiyam with ten verses, tonight

    "Whoever stands in prayer with ten verses is not written among the heedless."Abu Dawud Ustadh to check

    Start tonightBefore you sleep, pray two rakahs and recite ten verses. It takes about five minutes.

  • Fajr and 'Asr

    Guard Fajr and 'Asr, with full wudu in the cold

    "Whoever prays the two cool prayers will enter Paradise."Bukhari and Muslim Ustadh to check

    Start tomorrowSet your Fajr alarm tonight, and make your wudu complete, even when the water is cold.

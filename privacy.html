Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

The Winter Lock-In is a free online session run by Ilmi Online ("we"). This page explains what we do with the details you give us when you sign up. If you have a question, email support@ilmi.online.

What we collect

  • Your first name, email address and WhatsApp number, when you fill in the sign-up form.
  • Where you came from, for example which ad or link brought you to this page.

What we use it for

  • To save your seat and send you the confirmation, the reminders, the join link and the replay on WhatsApp.
  • To count how many people signed up and which ads or links worked.

We don't sell your details.

Who handles it for us

  • Chakra, our WhatsApp messaging provider, stores your sign-up and sends our messages through WhatsApp.
  • Vercel hosts this website.

Cookies and the Meta pixel

This site uses the Meta pixel to measure how many people sign up after seeing our ads, and to help Meta show our ads to people who are likely to be interested. The pixel sets cookies and shares limited browsing data with Meta, such as visiting this page and completing the sign-up. You can control cookies in your browser settings and your ad preferences in your Meta account.

Your choices

  • To stop the messages, reply to any of our WhatsApp messages to say so, or email us.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your details, to correct them or to delete them, at any time.
  • If you're in the UK and unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
